WHY STUDIO

Present live, capture it once, send it anywhere

Studio records the same window your investors would see — your deck or the live asset — with you narrating over it, so the video is the pitch, not a description of it.

Record the deck or the live asset

Open Studio on your slide deck or your interactive project and record exactly what's on screen — no separate screen-share tool.

Narrate over the video

Talk through the story as you record it. The narration is captured with the visuals, in the same take.

Turn on your webcam

Add a webcam picture-in-picture so viewers see you alongside the project, positioned in any corner of the frame.

Mark up and draw as you go

Circle a zone, box a target, or measure across the ground while you talk. The mark is written to the terrain, so it stays on the rock as the camera moves.

ONE RECORDING WINDOW

Everything you need is in the frame you're recording

Record, camera, markup tools and ink colours sit in a single dock at the bottom of the window — nothing to switch to, nothing running in the background. The dock sits in the letterbox band, so it stays on your screen and out of your video.

  • Record start / stopONE CLICK
  • Pen, area box, rulerBUILT IN
